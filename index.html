Word Game Knowledge

What is an Anagram?

An anagram is a word or phrase formed by rearranging the letters of a different word or phrase, using all the original letters exactly once. For instance, the letters in "cinema" can be rearranged to spell "iceman", and "silent" forms "listen".

Anagrams have been celebrated since ancient Greece, where poet Lycophron created anagrams of royal names. In modern word games—such as Scrabble, Words with Friends, Wordscapes, Jumble, and Wordle—anagramming is the core skill of looking at scrambled tiles on your rack and forming words with given letters to maximize your score.
