Last Updated: October 2026 • Effective Immediately

Key Takeaway: AnagramSolverHub does not use tracking cookies or third-party marketing beacons. We only use your browser's client-side localStorage to store your display and gameplay settings.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer by websites to identify your device across sessions. Local Storage is a modern, privacy-respecting browser feature that allows websites to remember your display preferences (like Dark Mode or sound toggles) directly on your device, without sending those details back to web servers with every page visit.

2. Exactly How We Use Local Storage

AnagramSolverHub uses exactly three (3) Local Storage items, strictly dedicated to saving your user preferences:

Storage Key Purpose Lifespan
asp_theme Saves your selected visual theme (dark or light). Persistent until cleared
asp_sound_enabled Remembers whether tactile tile click sound effects are turned on or muted. Persistent until cleared
asp_saved_words Stores word entries you starred in your private Favorites list. Persistent until cleared

3. Third-Party Cookies

We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, social media tracking pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts. Your browsing activity on AnagramSolverHub is completely private.

4. How to Manage or Clear Your Stored Data

You have full control over your local data. You can inspect or clear your saved data at any time through several methods:

  • Within the App: Open the "Saved Words" drawer from the top right and click Clear All to delete all bookmarked words.
  • In Your Browser Settings: You can delete local storage and site data for localhost or anagramsolverhub.com at any time under your browser's Privacy & Security preferences (e.g. Chrome Settings → Clear Browsing Data → Cookies and other site data).
